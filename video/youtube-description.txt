Title: Carry — From Receipt to Reimbursement | PayPal AI Hackathon

Carry helps small teams collect receipt evidence, apply reimbursement rules, record finance decisions, and track payment results in one traceable workflow.

This video shows the working local demo with synthetic receipts and explicitly simulated extraction and payments. No money moves. Real AI calls and PayPal Sandbox transactions remain to be verified; the implemented adapters are not presented as completed live integrations.
